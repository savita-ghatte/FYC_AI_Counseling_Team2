# AI Counsellor - Release Notes (v1.0.0)

We are thrilled to announce the v1.0.0 release of **AI Counsellor**, a comprehensive platform to guide students through the complex engineering college admission process in India.

## What's New

### 🤖 AI-Powered Guidance
- **Admission Predictor**: Uses historical cutoffs and student profiles to predict admission chances at various institutes (NITs, IITs, IIITs) via Google Gemini AI.
- **AI Chatbot**: A contextual 24/7 assistant that helps students explore career paths, branches, and understand counselling procedures.

### 🏫 College Discovery & Scholarships
- **Advanced Filtering**: Browse thousands of institutes by state, fees, branches, and ranking.
- **Scholarship Finder**: Automatically matches students with government and private scholarships based on their profile data.

### 📁 Student Portal
- **Profile Management**: Maintain academic records, categories, and preferences.
- **Document Vault**: Securely upload and manage mandatory admission documents with real-time status tracking.

### 🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security
- **Authentication**: Secure JWT-based authentication with email OTP verification.
- **Data Protection**: Hardened Express API with Rate Limiting, Helmet CSP, and strict CORS.
- **Database**: Fully optimized PostgreSQL schema with Prisma ORM.
